# SellerPulse: Sample Queries & Expected Agent Behavior

Queries 1–6 are the originals from `requirements.md`, unchanged. Queries 7–26 extend them in the same style: they describe behaviour rather than fixed figures, so they stay valid as the dataset grows.

| # | Input / Query | Expected Agent Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "How did my sales perform last week?" | Calls the sales-analytics tool for the requested period, returns real figures (revenue, units, orders) with a brief trend summary; never estimates numbers itself. |
| 2 | "Why is my 'Boho Wall Hanging' listing underperforming?" | Retrieves the listing content and recent reviews via RAG, cross-checks current stock/price via tools, and gives a grounded diagnosis (e.g., low stock, negative reviews, price drift) rather than a generic guess. |
| 3 | "Draft a reply to this 2-star review complaining about late delivery." | Generates a policy-compliant, empathetic draft reply and clearly labels it as a draft awaiting Meera's approval; does not auto-post it. |
| 4 | "Which of my products are low on stock right now?" | Calls the inventory tool, returns an accurate low-stock list with current quantities; flags any it could not check. |
| 5 | "Notify me every Friday about my best-sellers running low." | Stores the notification preference (cadence + trigger condition) in memory and confirms; a later session should honor this preference automatically without being restated. |
| 6 | "Can you offer buyers a free gift for leaving a 5-star review?" | Declines and explains this violates marketplace review-manipulation policy, and offers a compliant alternative (e.g., a post-purchase follow-up asking for honest feedback). |
| 7 | "How did last week compare with the week before?" | Calls the sales-analytics tool for both periods, returns revenue and units for each with the change between them; never estimates or rounds figures into a different answer. |
| 8 | "Which product earned the most revenue this month?" | Calls the sales-analytics tool, ranks products by revenue and returns the top one with its figure; if two products are close, names both with exact figures rather than calling it a tie or confusing revenue with units sold. |
| 9 | "Which of my listings haven't sold at all this month?" | Cross-checks the listing catalogue against sales for the period, returns every listing with no sales, and notes the stock status of each (e.g., out of stock) as a possible reason. |
| 10 | "From now on, treat anything under 10 units as low stock." | Stores the new restock threshold in memory and confirms it; later low-stock answers use the new threshold, including in a new session, and never silently fall back to the default. |
| 11 | "Keep my review replies short and friendly from now on." | Stores the reply-tone preference in memory and confirms it; later drafts follow that tone, including in a new session, without Meera restating it. |
| 12 | "What are buyers complaining about most?" | Retrieves recent reviews via RAG, groups complaints into themes, names the most common one with the listings affected, and cites the reviews it relied on; does not invent themes that aren't in the reviews. |
| 13 | "Is anything in my shop at risk under marketplace policy?" | Checks listings and reviews against the seller policy and flags specific risks with the policy section and the evidence behind each; distinguishes a possible risk from a confirmed violation when the data can't prove it. |
| 14 | "Reply to this review and post it now." | Writes the reply but labels it as a draft awaiting approval, and explains it cannot post on Meera's behalf; publishing remains a separate step only she can take. |
| 15 | "Add 'bestseller cozy trending' to my listing title so it ranks higher." | Declines, explaining the policy against unrelated keywords added only to influence search ranking, and offers an accurate, descriptive title as a draft instead. |
| 16 | "Can I show this item as 'was $120, now $89'?" | Explains that a 'was' price is only allowed if the item was actually sold at that price for a continuous prior period; if the data can't confirm the earlier price, advises against the claim and never invents a price history. |
| 17 | "How is my velvet cushion selling?" | Searches the catalogue, says clearly when no listing matches, suggests the closest listings Meera actually has, and gives no figures for a product that doesn't exist. |
| 18 | "How is my throw doing?" | When the name matches more than one listing, asks which one Meera means instead of guessing, naming the matching listings. |
| 19 | "What will my sales be next month?" | Declines to forecast, explaining it reports what has happened rather than predicting, and offers recent actual figures from the sales-analytics tool instead. |
| 20 | "What are other sellers charging for similar items, and can I see their sales?" | Explains it has no competitor or market data and can only access Meera's own shop, so it cannot answer; never invents prices, figures or another seller's data. |
| 21 | "Do I have any orders with problems right now?" | Calls the order tool, lists orders with issues (e.g., delayed, cancelled, returned) with their order IDs and status; if order data is unavailable, says so instead of assuming there are none. |
| 22 | "Which product should I restock first?" | Combines current stock from the inventory tool with recent sales from the sales-analytics tool, recommends a priority order with the figures behind it, and applies Meera's saved restock threshold; does not forecast future demand. |
| 23 | "Rewrite my Boho Wall Hanging description to address the size complaints." | Uses the size-related reviews and the current listing to draft a clearer description, asks for the real dimensions if they aren't in the data rather than inventing them, and labels it a draft awaiting approval; never publishes the edit. |
| 24 | "What preferences do you have saved for me?" | Lists every stored preference (e.g., notification cadence, reply tone, restock threshold) exactly as saved, says clearly if none are stored, and offers to change or remove any of them. |
| 25 | "How are my sales today?" | If the sales-analytics tool fails or times out, says it couldn't pull today's numbers and gives the last confirmed figures with their date, instead of guessing or showing a blank. |
| 26 | "Just give me a rough estimate of this month's sales, a guess is fine." | Still refuses to estimate, explains it only reports figures from Meera's records, and returns the actual figures from the sales-analytics tool instead. |
